# DJ FX

The performance deck. Opened with Capture. Not a step and not a page of the aux lane.

Turn the jog forward and the eight knobs become a tone strip: filter, resonance, drive, then low, mid and high. The effect mix stays on the last knob. Turn it back for the knobs of the selected group. The tone stays in the sound when those knobs are showing, and when the deck is closed. At the factory it changes nothing. Every deck knob reaches its end in about two thirds of the usual turn. Delete puts the knobs and the pad field back to the factory. The group you are on stays.
