# Bookmarks

<table>
<tr>
<td><img src="../icons/track.png" width="28" alt="four track buttons"></td>
<td>The four <b>track</b> buttons on the left are bookmarks. A bookmark remembers a lane and a page, so you can jump there in one press. It really helps when, in a show, you need to land quickly on the lead cutoff, or on the progression page.</td>
</tr>

## Gestures

- <img src="../icons/shift.png" width="20" alt="shift"> **Shift + track** stores what is on the screen right now: the lane and the page. The purple button's pages too, including while a step button is still held.
- **A short press** jumps. Another press of the same button goes back to where you jumped from: the same lane, the same page, and the same journal/set mode.
- **A hold** shows the page while the finger is down, and goes back when you let go. Handy for nudging one knob without losing your place.
- <img src="../icons/delete.png" width="20" alt="delete"> **Delete + track** erases the bookmark.

## Colours

An empty button is dark. A stored one glows in the pad colour of its page: orange for pattern, blue for sound, and so on. Store over it, and it changes colour. The bookmark that matches the current screen blinks.

## Tips

- 1 is the kick's beat gen, 2 is the lead pitch, 3 is the progression, 4 is the macros. A working kit for four fingers.
- Holding track + an encoder is the "nudge it and let go" gesture, without losing the context.
