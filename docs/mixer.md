# Mixer

One page: seven lane levels and master.

Each fader has a strip beside it. The bar is that channel's level, and a pixel above the bar is the peak. The peak holds, then falls. Reverb and delay show on the master strip only.

**Mute** plus a lane button toggles mute. Muted drums and notes go silent. Muted chords still feed Bass and Lead.

**Loop** plus a lane button locks that lane. Morph, Wander, Randomize, and macros leave it alone.
